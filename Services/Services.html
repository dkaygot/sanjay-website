Frequently Asked Questions

What is an archival print?
An archival print is a high-quality art or photographic print made with materials and processes designed to last a very long time without significant fading or deterioration.
How do I send my files for printing?
Files can be sent by email, Google Drive or wetransfer.com, or delivered in person to our address. Email: sanarpan@gmail.com
How do I prepare my files for printing?
Use the highest resolution possible.
  • Colour space: Adobe RGB or sRGB
  • File type: high-quality JPEG or TIFF, 8-bit or 16-bit (16-bit preferred)
  • Resolution: highest possible, at 300 DPI
What will be the border for prints?
Anything from 0.5 inches to 3 inches or more, depending on your requirement.
What is the maximum paper size of prints?
We currently print from A4 (8.27 × 11.69 inches) up to 24 inches on the shortest side (24 × 36 inches). Bigger prints are available on request.
What printer and inks will be used for printing?
Images are printed on the latest Epson printer with an extreme colour gamut, using UltraChrome PRO12 ink.
How will my prints be shipped?
All sheet prints up to A3+ (up to 13 × 19 inches) are individually placed in an archival clear bag and sent to you packed flat.

Anything larger than A2, such as roll prints, is wrapped in archival clear film and sent in a mailing tube, which protects better in transit. Allow your prints to lie flat for a few days before use or framing.
What happens to my digital files once printing is done?
Files are stored for 3 months after printing and are then automatically deleted from our system.
Do you provide framing services?
We do not provide framing ourselves, but we can assist you with framing services within Mumbai and Navi Mumbai.
